Creator options, built by tools/art/paint_character.py. Each option is on the boy and girl base in the front idle pose plus one side run frame, 3x. Any hair or clothing works on either body.
hair-shapes.png: 8 shapes on each body, with front, run and back: short, crop, spiky, shaggy, wavy, ponytail, bob, braids.
hats.png: none, pompom beanie, knit cap, baseball cap, hood, earmuffs.
tops.png: quilted jacket, parka with fur collar, puffer vest, long coat.
scarf.png: scarf off and on, as its own toggle.
bottoms.png: trousers, leggings, shorts over leggings, skirt over leggings.
shoes.png: snow boots, tall boots, sneakers.
skin.png: 8 tones, light to deep. Eyes.png: 8 colours.
colours-top, -hat, -bottoms, -shoes: the approved 30 colours. colours-hair: a new set of 30, 14 natural and 16 bright. boy and girl walk and run sheets: the two defaults.
Fixed: the boy's strings no longer show on his back. The girl's long coat now ends at the knee, so her legs show in the walk.
Skin tones are my own ramp, not LPC's named palettes. Eye colour barely shows at this size, so it is a subtle choice. Everything here is drawn in the tool; the game can't combine parts yet. That needs the creator build: combining the chosen layers into a sheet and copying one frame at a time to texture memory.
