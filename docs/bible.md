# PokeLegends of Nine DS: Story & World Bible (draft 1)

Draft for Kyle to mark up. Every name is a placeholder. Continues the story of *Poké Legends: Lands of Nine*
(the GBA game); nothing is copied from it but the story canon.

## Where Lands of Nine left off
Nine lands around one mountain. The player beat every Champion's hall, gathered all 18 plates, faced Arceus in
the Hall of Origin, and beat Kai, the Origin Champion. Team Tempest, the Tidewardens' legend, Wren (Vellorin's
Champion) and the eight Champions are all canon.

## The pitch
**Three years later.** From the top of the mountain, Arceus showed the player all nine lands. It never showed
what lies past them. When the plates came back together, the sea at the edge of the map drew back, and a tenth
land rose: **Lumeris**, a region Arceus did not make.

Lumeris is old, older than the plates. Its people built cities, bridges and towers over ruins of the ones who
lived there first, the **Ashen Choir**, who sealed something away before Arceus shaped the nine lands. A modern,
busy region in Black 2's style: big cities, long bridges, harbours, a desert highway, a snowbound observatory.

## Three threads
- **Team Unbound (villains).** They believe the nine lands are a cage Arceus built, and that Lumeris is the
  door out. They want to open the Ashen Choir's seal. Leader: **Sable Vane** (placeholder), once a researcher
  of the ruins.
- **The Ashen Choir (ancient mystery).** Murals in each gym town tell their story piece by piece. They didn't
  seal away a monster; they sealed away *the first creature*, older than Arceus, which Arceus has
  been keeping asleep ever since. (Final legendary: see open question 1.)
- **The rival: Rook** (placeholder). A Lumeris native who has never left and resents the "heroes from the nine
  lands". Starts hostile, joins you once Team Unbound shows its hand, becomes the Champion you face at the end.

## Returning faces (cameos, not the lead)
- **Kai:** shows up after badge 4, has heard of you, battles you once at a fixed level as a "test".
- **Wren:** runs a battle facility in the post-game.
- **Prof. Hawthorn:** sends you to Lumeris; your first contact is his former student, **Prof. Linden**, who gives
  you your starter.
- The player from Lands of Nine: a legend people talk about. Players never find out what happened to them
  (a door for a sequel).

## Player
A new trainer, any appearance (2 options, boy/girl, like Black 2). Lives in Lumeris's quiet southern harbour
town; the story starts when a ferry from the nine lands arrives for the first time.

## Game structure
- Black 2 style: 3D overworld with a tilted camera, 2D sprites, two-screen battles with a touch menu.
- Normal level scale (cap 100), not Lands of Nine's 900. A clean start for new players.
- Carried over from Lands of Nine: Nuzlocke mode (chosen at New Game) and a post-game tower.
- Post-game: Wren's battle facility, the Ashen Choir's sealed creature, a route back to Vellorin as a cameo area.
